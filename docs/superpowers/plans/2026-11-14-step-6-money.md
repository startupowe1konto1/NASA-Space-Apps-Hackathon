### F2: the margin chart

**Files:** create `src/steps/money/MarginChart.tsx`, test `src/steps/money/MarginChart.test.tsx`.

What it looks like (mobile first, 400 px wide; the same at 1280 px with more room):
- One row per rotation, in `moneyRows` order. On a phone the title is on its own line and the bar under it (the meter-list layout); from `sm:` up, title left and bar right.
- Title line: the rotation title, "Your rotation" for the current one, and on the right the average-year margin through `<SourcedNumber>` with unit `{currency}/ha`, 0 decimals (as `IncomeSection` does).
- Bar (height 24 px, a 44 px tall row so it is easy to tap):
  - track: `bg-slate-200`, full width, rounded;
  - range from the bad year (`low`) to the good year (`high`): options `bg-emerald-300 border border-emerald-700`, the current rotation `bg-slate-300 border border-slate-500`, same as `ScoreBar` uses;
  - marker at the average year: `bg-slate-900`, 6 px wide, 24 px tall, `ring-2 ring-white`;
  - a 1 px `bg-slate-400` zero line through all rows when the domain has negative values;
  - a dashed 1 px `border-slate-500` vertical line through all rows at the current rotation's average, labelled once under the axis "your rotation, average year". It lets the farmer see at a glance which option earns more than today.
- Axis under the last row: the ticks from `moneyScale` in `{currency}/ha`, `tabular-nums`, `text-sm text-slate-700`, light grid lines (`bg-slate-200`) behind the rows at each tick. One axis only.
- A rotation without money keeps its row: title, then a dashed empty track (`border-dashed border-slate-300`) and the reason in `text-slate-700` (`t(row.reason)`, e.g. "No yield response factor for oats, so its yield in dry years cannot be replayed."). Never hide the row and never draw a zero.
- No legend box: one colour for the options, grey for the current rotation, and every row is named. Text is slate, never green.
- Accessibility: each bar `role="img"` with an `aria-label` that reads the three numbers ("Field pea → Winter wheat → Potato + Phacelia: average year 5263 PLN per hectare, bad year 3710, good year 6323"); a visually hidden `<table>` with the same rows (rotation, bad year, average year, good year, confirmed payments) and a caption; the reference and zero lines are `aria-hidden`.
- Tests first: the marker's `left` is `pct(mean)`; the range spans `pct(low)` to `pct(high)`; the current row has the grey style and the reference line sits at its mean; a negative `low` draws the zero line; a `no-data` row shows its reason and no marker; the hidden table has one row per rotation with the same numbers. Wait for specific text (`findByText`), not just elements.
